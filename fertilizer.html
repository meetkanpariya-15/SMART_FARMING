Planting Techniques

These general planting tips can help with many crops.

  • Prepare the soil properly and ensure suitable drainage.
  • Use healthy seeds or seedlings suited to the local season.
  • Maintain crop-specific spacing and manage water carefully.
  • Monitor weeds, pests and crop growth regularly.
